Privacy Policy

Shorts Publisher (the "Tool") is a private desktop application. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the authorizing account (open ID, display name, avatar, profile link, bio, verification status).
  • Account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the list of the account's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Videos and captions that the account owner chooses to publish.

How data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner the performance of those videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The Tool has no server and does not upload data anywhere except to TikTok through its official API.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local data at any time and revoke the Tool's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Other users

The Tool does not collect data about any TikTok users other than the authorizing account owner.
