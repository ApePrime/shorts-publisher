Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher is a small private desktop tool used by one creator team to publish its own short videos to its own TikTok account and YouTube channel and to view statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) of those videos.

How it works

  • The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool.
  • The tool uploads a finished video from the owner's computer through the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft or as a direct post with the caption and privacy level chosen by the owner.
  • The tool reads the owner's profile counters and video list (Display API) to show performance of published videos.
  • For YouTube, the owner signs in with Google and authorizes the tool; it uploads the owner's videos to the owner's channel with the YouTube Data API and reads the channel's own statistics with the YouTube Analytics API.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other TikTok or YouTube users' data.
